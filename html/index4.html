The Glass House

The Glass House is a very modern and special building. It is made almost completely of glass! The walls, doors, and even some parts of the roof are glass, so you can see everything inside and outside.

The house was built by a famous architect named Philip Johnson in the United States. He wanted to make a simple but beautiful house where people could live close to nature. From inside the Glass House, you can see trees, grass, and the sky all around you.

The Glass House has one big room with a bed, a kitchen, and a bathroom. There are no walls between the rooms, so it feels open and bright. During the day, sunlight comes through the glass, and at night, you can see the stars.

Many visitors come to see the Glass House because it is very peaceful and creative. It shows that houses can be beautiful without big decorations. It is a perfect place for people who love nature and modern design.
